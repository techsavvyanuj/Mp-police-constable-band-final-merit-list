About MP Police Constable Band Final Merit List 2026

The MP Police Constable Band Final Merit List 2026 has been officially released by the Madhya Pradesh Police Recruitment Board (MPPRB) under Advertisement No. 11/2026. This merit list is for the post of Constable Band (PHQ Band / वाद्य) in Madhya Pradesh Police.


What is the MP Police PHQ Band Merit List?

The PHQ Band (Police Headquarters Band) is a specialised musical unit of Madhya Pradesh Police. The Final Merit List contains the names and merit ranks of all selected candidates for the Constable Band post based on their performance in the Written Exam, Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Document Verification rounds conducted by MPPRB.
